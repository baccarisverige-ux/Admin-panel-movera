# Notes for Claude

## "Add to later job"
When the owner says "add to later job" (or "note for later", "later job"):
1. Add the item to `LATER_JOB.md` at the root of this repo, as a `- [ ]` checkbox in the right section
   (Admin panel, Server, Payment provider, Driver app, Rider app, Still to discuss).
2. Commit and push it (branch `docs/later-job` until it is merged, then `main` via a small PR).
3. Tell the owner in one line what was added.
When an item is built, tick its box (`- [x]`).
